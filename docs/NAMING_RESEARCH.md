# Vixel Campaigns Naming Decision

Status: adopted for the web product on 2026-08-01.

This document records the product-naming decision. It is a market and brand
assessment, not a trademark clearance or legal opinion.

## Decision

- Product: `Vixel Campaigns`
- Descriptor: `AI Product-to-UGC Campaign Studio`
- Current workflow: `UGC Campaign`
- Differentiating engine: `Creative Router`
- Canonical origin: `https://ugc.vixelai.com`
- Primary category language: `AI UGC`, `product video ads`, `creator ads`, and
  `ad creative`

Recommended launch line:

> Turn product truth into creator ads ready to produce.

`Campaigns` is the product name. `Studio` is a category descriptor, not the
brand. `UGC` is a workflow and acquisition term, not the master brand. `KOC`
is a creator/distribution strategy inside a campaign.

## Why this name won

### It matches the product

The product turns one grounded brief into Creative Router directions, an
approved plan, an account-scoped cloud campaign, and, when billing/provider
readiness passes, traceable candidates and adoption receipts. The durable
object is a campaign, not a single generated clip.

### It can expand without overclaiming

`Vixel Campaigns` can contain UGC, product video, playable, cinematic, and
future campaign workflows. It does not claim media buying, publishing, budget
management, or autonomous optimization that the current private beta does not
provide.

### It behaves like a product name

`Vixel Campaigns` is shorter and more natural in product and social language
than `Vixel Campaign Studio`: “Built with Vixel Campaigns” and “Open in Vixel
Campaigns.” The full `vixelcampaigns` string also fits within X's 15-character
username limit, although public profile availability must be confirmed while
signed in and is not guaranteed by a public 404 response.

### Search intent stays in the descriptor

The brand does not need to carry every keyword. Titles, headings, guides, and
workflow pages can accurately use `AI UGC`, `AI video ads`, `product video`,
and `ad creative` while the product retains a distinct, reusable name.

## Market and X language

The category's public language converges on outcomes rather than “marketing
studio” as a universal term:

- [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/) leads with “AI Ads” and “AI Ad Platform.”
- [Arcads](https://www.arcads.ai/) leads with creating winning ads with AI and
  uses `UGC Studio` as a feature, not its platform category.
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/tool/ai-video-ad-generator) uses `AI Video Ad
Generator` and treats UGC as one supported format.
- [Captions](https://captions.ai/tools/ai-ad-generator) uses `AI Ads` for its
  product-to-ad workflow.
- [AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai/) uses `AI Ad Creative Generator`
  and keeps UGC as one generation feature.
- [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-automation) uses `Marketing
Studio` as its own product packaging for a much broader end-to-end creative
  automation promise.

X launch and practitioner posts likewise favor `AI ads`, `video ads`, `UGC
ads`, `ad creative`, `hooks`, `variations`, and `workflows`. Examples include
the [Higgsfield launch post embedded in this X
thread](https://x.com/knoxtwts/status/2043940502521643020) and an [Arcads
workflow discussion](https://x.com/rom1trs/status/2036083539293171888).

The implication is deliberate: use those phrases in acquisition copy, but do
not turn a crowded category phrase into the only brand asset.

## Name-collision boundary

`Vixel` already has meaningful collision risk in software and AI video:

- The iPhone app [Vixel – AI Video
  Generator](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vixel-ai-video-generator/id6756965785)
  is published by FENIX MOBILE YAZILIM A.S. It is not this product.
- [vixel.tech](https://vixel.tech/) and [vixel.hu](https://www.vixel.hu/) offer
  unrelated AI image/video or advertising capabilities.
- [Vixel Creative Studio](https://vixelcreativestudio.com/) is an unrelated
  active creative business.
- A public trademark-data mirror reports EUTM `018191540` for the word `Vixel`
  in software/SaaS classes. The record must be confirmed in the [official EUIPO
  search](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip), followed by a qualified
  search in every launch market.

The product suffix reduces ambiguity but does not cure a master-brand conflict.
Before broad paid promotion, app publication, or expansion into the EU, UK,
US, or Turkey, obtain a formal trademark and likelihood-of-confusion review.

## App identity and acquisition rules

The unrelated FENIX app can create web-search spillover, but there is no direct
App Store traffic path without publisher cooperation. Vixel Campaigns must not
present itself as that app's website, account portal, support service, or
subscription manager.

Allowed acquisition surfaces:

- one factual, indexable identity-comparison page;
- generic intent pages for AI UGC ads, product video ads, approval workflows,
  and source-grounded campaign planning;
- a future written partnership with the publisher for an in-app/export CTA;
- a separately owned companion iOS app if direct App Store acquisition becomes
  strategically important.

Do not use the third-party icon, screenshots, ratings, prices, App Store badge,
or language that implies affiliation. Accounts, subscriptions, purchases,
uploads, data, and support are separate.

## Naming guardrails

- Keep legacy technical identifiers stable, including export formats,
  database schema names, cookies, local-storage keys, redirects, and the
  existing Vercel project.
- Use `Vixel Campaigns` consistently in visible brand and metadata fields.
- Use `UGC Campaign` for the current workflow.
- Use `Creative Router` for the routing engine, not as a second visible agent.
- Describe the product as an account-based private beta with account-scoped
  cloud campaigns and browser/JSON recovery paths.
- Report account auth, cloud campaigns, billing, and live generation as
  independent deployment capabilities; never infer one from another.
- Keep billing and live-generation readiness statements visible until account,
  entitlement, provider, deployment flag, and isolated-ledger checks all pass.
- Do not claim ad performance, customer experience, ROI, or real social proof
  from AI-generated creator media.
